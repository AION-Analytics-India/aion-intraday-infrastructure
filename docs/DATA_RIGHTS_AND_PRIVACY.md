# Data rights, eligible users and privacy

**Public product policy and evaluation notice, 12 November 2026. Legal review is required before incorporating these terms into customer contracts.**

## No raw market-data redistribution

AION does not sell, license onward, sublicense, rent, give away, publish, distribute, redistribute, broadcast, sample, or provide access to raw exchange tick data, quote streams, depth/order book data or private broker market-data feeds. This restriction applies to paid and unpaid arrangements, samples, proof-of-concept activity, testing, trials, demonstrations and resale.

Market data is sourced by the customer under independent contractual authority, and must be used only for the purpose, user class, venue, geography, processing mode, output type, retention period and downstream access permitted by the relevant source agreement.

Transforming or aggregating market data does not automatically remove exchange, vendor or broker rights. Derived outputs, non-display processing, algorithmic use and onward delivery may require additional authorization. AION will not deliver an output for which the parties cannot establish the required rights.

Primary policy reference: https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy
Non-display information: https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/non-display-data

## Intended professional access

The institutional leasing service is not a consumer product. It is evaluated for professional developers, eligible technology integrators, verified SEBI-registered Research Analysts and Investment Advisers and authorized institutional or broker technology teams. AION may require evidence of registrations, entity identity, use case, data-source agreements and relevant permissions.

Developer access to the separately published standalone IMI API does not grant institutional signal delivery, broker access, trading rights or reseller status. Verification of SEBI registration is not blanket permission to give research, advice or algorithmic trading services through AION.

## Privacy by design, with release gates

Customer-facing deployment designs contemplate separate tenant identity, isolation of compute and state, scope-limited credentials, encrypted storage and transit where appropriate, minimal retention, auditability, restricted operator access and signed deletion/return procedures.

Not every proposed deployment is currently certified. No statement in this repository guarantees that every deployment provides zero visibility, zero retention, absolute privacy, absence of vulnerabilities or compliance with every data-residency regime.

White-Box clients retain their proprietary detector source code. If they elect to use an optional Edge review, only specified candidate metadata is transmitted for the gate. Exact metadata, logging and retention are negotiated in the customer contract.

## Restricted methods and intellectual property

This documentation does not license AION's strategy logic, model parameters, weights, research methods, internal configurations, source code, market-data entitlements or branding. All such rights depend on separate written agreements and actual chain of title.

## Regulatory and performance notice

No product statement here constitutes investment advice, regulated research, a credit rating, guaranteed return, SEBI/exchange approval, consent to redistribute exchange data or contractual commitment to execute trades. No operational SLA exists without a separate written SLA supported by acceptance evidence.
