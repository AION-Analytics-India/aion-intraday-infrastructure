# Deployment and qualification

## Customer pathways

1. **Standalone IMI:** Caller-supplied authorized headlines. Separate published API subscription and user entitlement. No leased institutional signal feed.
2. **Research/Replay:** Proposed historical Edge analysis using specifically licensed recordings and isolated replay state.
3. **Intraday IMI:** Proposed licensed live-news ingestion, event interpretation and streaming.
4. **Live Edge:** Proposed integration of an approved Edge gate with permitted customer workflows.
5. **White-Box:** Processed Greeks/indicators; client retains own proprietary strategy detector and may request a co-located second gate.
6. **Full Engine:** Rust compute, five public strategy family categories, Edge Heuristics, native ML and an authorized signal stream.

## Deployment options

- AION Hosted: isolated dedicated tenant resources and approved restricted network access. Availability and isolation have to be certified.
- BYOC: approved image installed in a customer-controlled AWS, Azure or GCP network. Security, region, rollback and image signing require acceptance.
- On-Premise: approved customer Linux or Mac Studio system with contracted rights and measured latency, support and recovery.

These options are proposed deployment models, not live certifications or a guarantee of sub-millisecond execution.

## Eligibility review

The customer supplies entity documentation, professional use-case description, validated RA/IA registration where applicable, broker/data licenses, desired instrument and market scope, network/latency needs, and intended user access. AION reviews rights, outputs and technical readiness before any lease can be contracted.

## Commercial language

The proposed INR 500,000/month Full Engine baseline and the proposed INR 249,000/month institutional IMI entry price are indicative and non-orderable. One-time integration work, tenant compute, support and external licensed data costs may be additional. Full pricing and SLAs require a signed agreement.

## Responsibility allocation

The customer controls its authorized market-data contract, downstream trading decisions, any brokerage order submission, investment advice and supervision obligations. AION supplies only modules and outputs approved under the relevant contract and rights matrix.
